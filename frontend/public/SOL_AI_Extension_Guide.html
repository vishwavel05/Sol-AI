1. How to Download & Install the Extension
1
Download Files: Click Download சொல் AI Extension on the website to receive both the ZIP bundle and this PDF guide.
2
Extract Archive: Right-click sol-ai-extension.zip and select Extract All to an easily accessible folder on your computer.
3
Open Extensions Page: In Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Brave, navigate to chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions).
4
Enable Developer Mode: Toggle the Developer mode switch at the top-right corner of the page to ON.
5
Load Extension: Click "Load unpacked" in the top-left corner and select the extracted extension folder. The சொல் AI emblem will appear in your toolbar!
2. How to Use on Any Webpage

🔍 Instant Highlight & Lookup

Highlight any Tamil word or compound phrase on any webpage (news portals, Wikipedia, blogs). Click the floating சொல் AI badge to open the inspection drawer.

🧩 FOMA FST Morphology

Deconstructs complex agglutinative words into their base lemma, grammatical case, number, and suffix attachments with zero guesswork.

🎯 Deterministic Contextual WSD

Disambiguates polysemous words (e.g., கால் as fraction vs. anatomical foot) based on the exact enclosing sentence context.

📜 Classical Literary Grounding

Retrieves verified historical couplets and verses from Sangam literature (Tirukkural, Purananuru, Manimekalai, Silappatikaram) without hallucination.

3. System Limitations & Usage Policy

⚠️ 20 Requests Per Day Usage Policy

  • Daily Rate Limit: To ensure equitable access and protect shared cloud infrastructure, each user is allotted a policy limit of 20 requests per day.
  • AWS & Free-Tier AI APIs: The backend is deployed on an AWS EC2 instance connected to free-tier LLM endpoints (Google Gemini & Groq).
  • Responsible Use: Please use lookups thoughtfully, allow 2–3 seconds between queries, and avoid automated scripts or rapid clicking.
  • Deterministic Safe Fallback: If third-party AI rate limits are exceeded, SOL AI automatically falls back to our offline dictionary & Sangam database engine.
